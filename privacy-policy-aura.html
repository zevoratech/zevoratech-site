Aura TV Launcher Privacy Policy

App: Aura TV Launcher · Package: in.zevoratech.aura · Developer: ZevoraTech, Perambalur, Tamil Nadu, India
Last updated: 18 November 2026

In short

Aura is a launcher for Android TV and Google TV. It has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising and no analytics or tracking tools. We do not collect, store on our servers, sell or share any personal data. Everything Aura remembers stays on your TV.

1. What Aura reads on your TV

To build your home screen, Aura reads from your TV, on the device only:

  • the list of installed apps that can be opened, with their names, icons and banners (Aura also uses the artwork to pick the screen colours)
  • the list of TV inputs, such as HDMI, so the Inputs shortcuts can open them

This information is used on the TV to draw the screen and is never sent anywhere.

2. What Aura stores

Aura saves the following in its private storage on your TV:

  • your settings, such as which parts of the home screen are switched on, your chosen colour and the animation setting
  • the order of your apps and the apps you hid
  • your three most recently opened apps
  • a list of apps you removed with Advanced removal, so you can restore them

Nothing in this list identifies you. It stays on the TV and is not backed up or uploaded by Aura.

3. Permissions

  • Delete packages: lets you uninstall apps you installed yourself. Android shows its own confirmation every time.
  • Internet: used for one optional feature only, Advanced removal (see section 4). Aura does not use it to reach any server on the internet or any other device.

4. Advanced removal

Advanced removal is optional and off until you set it up. It lets you remove pre-installed apps that Android does not normally allow a launcher to remove. It works only if you switch on USB debugging in your TV's Developer options and accept the TV's “Allow debugging?” prompt.

Aura then talks to your TV's own debugging service at the address 127.0.0.1, which is the TV itself. The key Aura creates for this stays in its private storage. An app is removed only after you choose it and confirm, important system apps are protected, and removed apps can be restored from the Restore list. ZevoraTech has no access to your TV.

5. Data we collect and share

None. Aura does not collect, transmit, sell or share personal information, and it contains no advertising, analytics or third-party code that collects data.

Google Play may give us anonymous crash and performance reports (Android vitals) for devices whose owners chose to share diagnostics with Google. We cannot identify anyone from them, and they are handled under Google's own privacy policy.

6. Links to our website

The About screen shows a link to www.zevoratech.in. It opens only if you choose it, and it opens in your TV's browser, outside Aura.

7. Your choices and deleting your data

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to delete. To remove what Aura saved on your TV, clear Aura's data in your TV's app settings, or uninstall Aura. You can restore apps removed with Advanced removal at any time from the Restore list.

8. Children

Aura is a general utility for TV owners and is not directed at children. It does not collect information from anyone, including children.

9. Changes to this policy

If Aura starts handling data differently, we will update this page and the date at the top before the change reaches you. Other ZevoraTech apps have their own separate policies.
