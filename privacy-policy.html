Privacy Policy

Website: zevoratech.in · Owner: ZevoraTech · Effective date: 24 September 2026

In short: This website does not run ads or analytics, and does not use tracking cookies. The only personal information it handles is what you choose to type into the contact form, which is sent to us by email. Our apps handle data differently from this website and each other — see the app-specific policies linked at the bottom of this page.

1. Who we are

ZevoraTech is a software studio operating this website at zevoratech.in. For any question about this policy, or about how we handle your information, contact us at info@zevoratech.in.

2. The contact form

When you submit the contact form on this site, the name, email address, topic, and message you enter are sent to our inbox using Web3Forms, a third-party form-delivery service. Web3Forms processes that submission only to route it to us by email — we do not use it for any other service, and we don't ask it to store your submissions beyond what's needed to deliver that email. We use the information you send only to reply to you about your enquiry, and we don't add it to any marketing list without your separate consent.

3. The "notify me" field

The homepage includes a field inviting you to leave your email to hear about new product launches. At present this field is a placeholder: it does not transmit or store the email address you enter anywhere. If we turn this into a real mailing list in future, we'll update this section first to explain how that data is handled and how to unsubscribe.

4. Hosting and technical logs

This website is hosted on Cloudflare Pages and served through Cloudflare's network. Like almost every website, the underlying infrastructure automatically logs standard technical data for security and performance purposes — things like IP address, browser type, and request timestamps. ZevoraTech does not separately collect or review this data; it exists as part of Cloudflare's normal infrastructure logging, governed by Cloudflare's privacy policy.

5. Cookies, analytics, and advertising

This website does not use tracking cookies, third-party analytics scripts, or advertising of any kind. We don't build a profile of visitors or track you across other sites.

6. Third-party links

Pages on this site link out to services such as the Google Play Store and Google Drive for our apps. Once you leave zevoratech.in, that service's own privacy policy applies, not this one.

7. Children's privacy

This website is a company website intended for a general business audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children through this site.

8. Changes to this policy

If how this website handles data changes, we'll update this page and the effective date above.

9. Contact us

Questions about this policy: info@zevoratech.in
