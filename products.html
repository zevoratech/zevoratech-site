Factory Operations App

An Android application built for a manufacturing client to run daily floor operations from one place — production tracking, sales management, cash handling, and staff attendance via punch-in/punch-out. Used daily by supervisors and staff on site, with periodic updates from us as their needs evolve.

Completed & handed over Manufacturing · Android app

Factory Stock Management App

An Android app built for the same manufacturing client to track inventory and stock levels across the factory floor — keeping stock counts accurate and easy to check from the floor, not just from an office desk.

Completed & handed over Healthcare · Workflow automation

Automated Leave Approval System

A leave request system built for a hospital client so doctors can submit advance leave requests through a simple form. Each request is checked automatically against nine internal policy conditions, with instant auto-approval when all are met — replacing a manual back-and-forth with a process that takes seconds.

Completed & handed over Manufacturing · Company website

Corporate Website

A company website designed and built for a plastics manufacturing client — covering their story, product range, and a clear way for buyers to get in touch. Live and public-facing today.
